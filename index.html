Welcome toThe Hague region.

New in the Netherlands? We help international newcomers live, work and study in The Hague region. All our services are free.

  • City Hall, Spui 70
  • Monday to Thursday, 9:00–17:00

What brings you here?

Choose your situation. Each guide explains what you need to arrange, in what order, and who can help you.

  • Highly skilled migrant You work for a multinational, an innovative company, a university or an NGO.
  • European migrant worker You work in the Netherlands and have an EU or EEA nationality.
  • Family migrant You want to live here with your partner or a family member.
  • Student You study at a university or college in The Hague, Leiden or Delft.
  • Privileged or diplomat You work for an embassy, a consulate or an international organisation.
  • Refugee You asked for asylum in the Netherlands because you need protection.
Hiring from abroad?Support for companies with international staff, or thinking of hiring. Guide for employers

Three formalities. One desk.

Are you a highly skilled migrant, scientific researcher, diplomat, graduate or start-up founder? We arrange your first government formalities at our desk in City Hall, for you and your family.

  1. Register with the municipality

    In The Hague, Delft, Rijswijk or Leidschendam-Voorburg. Your address is then officially recorded.

  2. Get your citizen service number (BSN)

    You need your BSN to work, open a bank account, get health insurance and pay taxes.

  3. Collect your residence permit

    Pick up your residence and work documents from the IND, the Immigration and Naturalisation Service.

Get to know the region

A city by the sea, historic towns and green dunes. And The Hague itself: the international city of peace and justice.

  • Live Housing, healthcare, schools and the beach: everything for daily life.
  • Work Find a job, understand your contract and taxes, and grow your career.
  • Study Universities in The Hague, Leiden and Delft, student housing and grants.
  • Do business Start, invest in or grow your company, with support for international entrepreneurs.
